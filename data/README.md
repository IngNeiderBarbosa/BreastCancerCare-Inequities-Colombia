# Data sources

This study used three independent publicly available Colombian datasets for empirical parameterisation of the computational model.

The datasets were not linked at the individual level and were not treated as a single longitudinal cohort.

## Bogotá

Source: Secretaría Distrital de Salud de Bogotá.

Dataset:
**Cáncer de mama - Datos Abiertos Bogotá**

Purpose in this study:
- empirical characterisation of structural and temporal variables;
- insurance-scheme distribution;
- diagnostic and treatment timeliness;
- selected pairwise associations used for synthetic population parameterisation.

## Santiago de Cali

Source: Secretaría de Salud de Santiago de Cali.

Dataset:
**Casos confirmados del evento cáncer de mama notificados en Santiago de Cali**

Purpose in this study:
- characterisation of diagnostic and treatment timeliness;
- insurance categories;
- territorial heterogeneity;
- complementary descriptive analyses.

## Socorro, Santander

Source: Hospital Regional Manuela Beltrán III Nivel.

Dataset:
**Datos Básicos Examen de Mama**

Purpose in this study:
- reproductive and clinical attribute distributions;
- age-related patterns;
- selected associations involving breastfeeding, offspring, and breast masses.

## Important note

These datasets originate from independent public repositories and represent different populations, periods, and data structures.

They were integrated only at the model-parameterisation level. No record linkage was performed.

Original datasets remain subject to the licences, terms of use, and update policies established by their respective data providers.

Exact dataset identifiers, access dates, and archived versions used in the final analysis will be documented in the reproducibility release.
